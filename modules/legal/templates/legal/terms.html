1. What COLLABoard is

COLLABoard is a free, real-time whiteboard for classes. Teachers create sessions; students join with a code to watch, draw (when the teacher allows it), chat and share files.

COLLABoard is built and run by a team of students (“we”, “us”) as a course project (CSIT327, Group 4). It is not an official service of Cebu Institute of Technology – University, and the university is not responsible for it.

Because it is a student project, features may change and the service may be paused or shut down at any time, for example when the course ends. If we plan to shut it down, we will try to give notice so you can download your data.
