FAQs & Help

Welcome to the FAQs and Help Page! Here you’ll find guides, tips, and quick explanations to help you navigate and use the system smoothly.

1. Why can’t I draw immediately after joining a session?

You need to wait for the teacher to grant you drawing permissions. Once approved, the drawing tools will be enabled for you.

2. How do I join a session?

You can join either by scanning the session’s QR code or by entering the six-character session code.

3. Where can I view the sessions I’ve joined?

Click “My Dashboard” on the sidebar to view the list of all sessions you have joined.

4. How do I log out of my account?

Click “Log out” at the bottom of the sidebar, or use the Logout card in Settings.

5. What is the difference between My Profile and Settings?

My Profile shows your username and email, and lets you change your email. Settings is where you change your password, download a copy of your data, read the Terms and Privacy Policy, or delete your account.

6. Is my attendance immediately recorded once I join a session?

Yes. Your entry is logged automatically, and the teacher can see when you joined and when you were last active.

7. Do I stay logged in after changing my password?

Yes, on the device you used to change it. Any other device or browser where you were logged in is signed out, so use your new password there.

8. Can I download my session content?

Yes. During a session, click “Export PNG” at the top of the whiteboard to download the current board as an image. Teachers can also download saved boards as PNG or PDF from Saved Sessions.

9. Can I upload or insert photos during a session?

Yes. Use the “Upload” button (images, PDF, DOCX or PPTX up to 10 MB). Images are placed on the whiteboard; other files appear as links under the board. Students need drawing permission to upload.

10. As a teacher, how can I create a session?

Go to your dashboard and select “Manage Sessions.” Enter a session title, then click “Create Session.” On the session list, click “Invite” to show a QR code students can scan; they can also type the session code shown next to the title.

Need more help? We’d be happy to assist you!
